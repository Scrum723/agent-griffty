# Agent Griffty — Music Rights Intelligence Module

## Pitch
Agent Griffty is the first autonomous financial operating system for independent music creators. While most agents help you create content, Griffty manages the business. It autonomously monitors DistroKid royalties, watches for Content ID false claims, scouts DISCO.fm for relevant sync deals, tracks TikTok Sound UGC revenue, and auto-posts music releases across all platforms. Designed with a strict zero-capital constraint and a mandatory human gate on deal approvals, Griffty empowers independent artists to protect and monetize their IP without needing a label or manager. It builds a self-sustaining financial loop, routing recovered royalties and new sync income directly into targeted ad campaigns to grow the artist's audience.

## Yamaha Music Innovations Fund Alignment
Griffty perfectly aligns with the Yamaha Music Innovations Fund by democratizing access to professional-grade rights management and financial operations. It directly empowers independent artists to take control of their IP, maximize their revenue, and scale their careers without sacrificing equity or relying on traditional industry gatekeepers.

## Founder Story
I'm Charles Clottin, a solo developer and independent creator. Along with Doc Weather, a WNY-based musician and meteorologist, we experienced firsthand the administrative nightmare of being an independent artist. Between tracking down missing royalties, fighting false Content ID claims, and trying to find sync placements, there was no time left to actually make music. I built Griffty to solve our own problem: we needed a tireless, autonomous business manager that could handle the financial operations and rights management, freeing us up to be creators again.
