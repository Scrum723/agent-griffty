# Agent Griffty: Pitch Deck Outline (Swimming with Narwhals)

## Slide 1: Title
- **Agent Griffty**
- The autonomous financial OS for independent music creators.
- Presenter: Charles Clottin

## Slide 2: The Problem
- Independent musicians spend 70% of their time on administration, not music.
- Royalties are fragmented, Content ID false claims steal revenue, and finding sync deals is a full-time job.
- Hiring a business manager or label costs 15-50% of revenue.

## Slide 3: The Solution
- **Agent Griffty:** An autonomous, always-on business manager.
- Monitors DistroKid royalties, disputes Content ID claims, and scouts DISCO.fm sync deals.
- Zero-capital constraint: only uses found money to fund audience growth (ads).

## Slide 4: Demo / How it Works
- Visual walkthrough of a 15-minute cycle.
- Griffty finds a missing royalty → queues it for approval → recovers funds → routes to Facebook Ads to promote a new single.
- Human-in-the-loop: The artist always approves major deals.

## Slide 5: Market Size
- The Indie Creator Economy is exploding.
- Millions of DIY artists uploading 100k+ tracks daily to streaming platforms.
- Massive uncaptured value in uncollected royalties and unmonetized UGC on TikTok/YouTube.

## Slide 6: Business Model
- SaaS subscription for independent creators (e.g., $20/month).
- No percentage of royalties taken—artists keep 100% of their IP and earnings.

## Slide 7: Music Tech Differentiation
- Not another generative AI music creation tool.
- Not a traditional distributor.
- The first autonomous *agentic* workflow designed specifically for music rights and revenue operations.
