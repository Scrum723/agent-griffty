## Working title
*Unpaid minutes in a watch: who eats the cost when lake-effect and heat hit the warehouse*

## 150-word pitch

Last-mile and warehouse labor in the Buffalo–Rochester corridor runs on thin margins and public weather. NWS and SPC already timestamp watches and warnings. Workers often do not. This project follows the money: who profits when a shift continues through a lake-effect warning, who pays when an ankle or a wreck takes a driver out, and which public forecast products never reach the floor. I am an independent WNY weather journalist (Doc Weather) who also worked that floor and spent August 2026 injured. The fellowship would fund records, payroll/attendance pattern reporting (no stolen data), and a published investigation with a U.S. outlet. Not a memoir of my bills — a business story about weather as an unpriced labor input.
