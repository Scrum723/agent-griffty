# IP Protection Strategy

## 1. DistroKid Content ID Enrollment (YouTube Money)
1. Log into the DistroKid dashboard.
2. Navigate to the specific release/track.
3. Scroll to the "Extras" section and select "YouTube Money" (Content ID).
4. Review the terms (20% fee to DistroKid for identified content).
5. Confirm that the track contains 100% original audio and no uncleared samples.
6. Submit the enrollment.

## 2. ISRC Audit Checklist
For every track, verify the following in the DistroKid dashboard:
- [ ] Track has a successfully generated ISRC (International Standard Recording Code).
- [ ] ISRC matches the code registered with BMI/ASCAP.
- [ ] Track title and artist name exactly match across all platforms.
- [ ] Audio file length and format are correct.

## 3. BMI Work Registration Process
1. Log into the BMI Online Services portal.
2. Navigate to "Works Registration".
3. Select "Register New Work".
4. Enter the work title, writers (with their IPI numbers), and publisher information.
5. Enter the split percentages (must total 100%).
6. Link the ISRC (if available).
7. Submit the registration.

**CSV Template Structure for Bulk Upload:**
`Work Title, Writer 1 Name, Writer 1 IPI, Writer 1 Share %, Publisher 1 Name, Publisher 1 IPI, Publisher 1 Share %, ISRC, ISWC`

## 4. Facebook/Instagram Rights Manager Enrollment
1. Ensure the track is distributed to Facebook/Instagram via DistroKid.
2. DistroKid automatically opts eligible tracks into Facebook/Instagram monetization if selected during upload.
3. Verify in the DistroKid "Bank" or "Extras" tab that Social Media monetization is active.
4. For direct management, apply for Meta Rights Manager through the Facebook Page settings (requires approval).

## 5. TikTok Sound Commerce Enrollment
1. Distribute the track to TikTok via DistroKid.
2. Ensure the "TikTok/ByteDance" store is selected.
3. In TikTok, link the sound to your official artist account (Sound Page -> Claim).
4. Monitor TikTok analytics for sound usage and trends.

## 6. Spotify for Artists Verification Checklist
- [ ] Claim your artist profile via `artists.spotify.com`.
- [ ] Upload a high-quality avatar and header image.
- [ ] Update the artist bio and include social links.
- [ ] Select an "Artist Pick" (latest release or playlist).
- [ ] Submit upcoming releases to Spotify editorial playlist consideration (at least 2 weeks prior).

```text
The information in this notification is accurate, and under penalty of perjury, I am the owner, or an agent authorized to act on behalf of the owner, of an exclusive right that is allegedly infringed.

Please remove or disable access to the infringing material immediately.

Sincerely,

[Your Name/Digital Signature]
[Your Contact Information/Email]
```

## 8. YouTube Content ID Dispute Letter Template
```text
Subject: Content ID Claim Dispute - [Your Video Title]

I am disputing the Content ID claim on my video titled "[Your Video Title]" (URL: [Video URL]). 

The claimed content, "[Track Title]", is my original creation. I am the sole copyright owner and have not granted exclusive rights to any third party that would preclude my use of the content on my own channel. 

Alternatively, if this is a false claim by a third party, please note that I am the original creator and hold all necessary rights to this material.

Please release this claim immediately.

Thank you,
[Your Name/Channel Name]
```

## 9. Monitoring Cadence
Griffty performs the following automated checks:
- **Weekly:** 
  - Scan YouTube, TikTok, and Instagram for unauthorized re-uploads of high-performing tracks.
  - Check DistroKid dashboard for any rejected uploads or warning flags.
- **Monthly:** 
  - Cross-reference new releases with BMI registrations to ensure no missing works.
  - Audit Spotify/Apple Music profiles for missing bio links or outdated images.
  - Review Content ID dispute statuses.
- **Quarterly:** 
  - Full ISRC audit across all distributed platforms.
  - Royalty statement reconciliation (DistroKid bank vs. expected platform payouts).
  - Update DMCA and rights management templates if platform policies change.
